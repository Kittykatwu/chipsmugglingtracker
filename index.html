AI Chip Smuggling Enforcement Tracker

Tracking what happens when people are caught smuggling advanced AI chips to China.

The United States restricts exports of the most advanced AI chips to China, including Nvidia's data-center GPUs such as the A100, H100, H200 and Blackwell-generation B200 and B300, and other governments have begun acting against people who route them there through third countries. This project tracks the public court cases and official actions that follow: who has been charged, where, with what, and what happened next. Each case and defendant is recorded from court records and official statements so that reporters and researchers can cite precise, sourced numbers about the enforcement response.

Chipmakers such as Nvidia are named here to describe the hardware involved. A company is a defendant only if it is listed as one.
