Before you quote

  • Charges are allegations. Every defendant is presumed innocent unless proven guilty.
  • Status reflects the latest step found in public sources, with its date. "Not found" does not mean "did not happen".
  • Never add dollar figures with different bases. The only totals are within one basis.
  • Read the methodology for scope, limitations, and how the US count compares with the government's own figure.

This tracker compiles public court records and official statements for research. It is not legal advice. Inclusion does not imply guilt.
