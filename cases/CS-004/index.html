Partially resolved Some defendants resolved (plea, sentence), others pending.

Charges are allegations. Every defendant is presumed innocent unless proven guilty.

Defendant notes

  • Yih-Shyan 'Wally' Liaw: Pleaded not guilty to Counts 1-3 on 1 Apr 2026. Trial originally set for 2 Nov 2026; on 10 Jun 2026 the court reset jury selection and trial for 29 Mar 2027 (docket). Rule 12 motions briefed in autumn 2026. The $5M bond is from N.D. Cal. reporting and was not confirmed on the S.D.N.Y. docket.
  • Ruei-Tsang 'Steven' Chang: DOJ (19 Mar 2026): Chang "remains a fugitive." No arrest or appearance on the S.D.N.Y. docket as of its 20 Nov 2026 update.
  • Ting-Wei 'Willy' Sun: Waived indictment and pleaded guilty to all four counts of the S1 information on 8 Oct 2026 under a plea agreement (docket entry 71; not reviewed). Obstruction count: deleted an encrypted messaging app and its contents on 19 Mar 2026, the day of arrests. The information includes a forfeiture allegation. Cooperation terms, if any, are not public.

Notes, disputes and open questions

$510M is the value explicitly alleged to have reached China (Apr-May 2025). Bloomberg reported the pass-through company is OBON (Bangkok), which is not charged. Super Micro is not a defendant; it says it was a victim and an internal probe found no evidence current senior management knew. Indictment filed 17 Mar 2026 and unsealed 19 Mar 2026 (docket). The DOJ release refers to "the U.S. Manufacturer" and does not name Super Micro.
