Defendant notes

  • Hon Ning 'Mathew' Ho: Judgment (22 Sep 2026): 57 months in prison on Counts One and Nine, concurrent; 36 months of supervised release; fine waived; $200 special assessment. The court denied his motion for a downward variance. A forfeiture order was entered the same day. No DOJ release found.
  • Brian Curtis Raymond: A job offer as CTO of an AI cloud company was rescinded after the charges.
  • Cham 'Tony' Li: His lawyer withdrew on 28 Oct 2026.

Notes, disputes and open questions

Raymond is described in the indictment as owner of a distributor licensed to sell Nvidia GPUs. DOJ states no value for the 400 exported A100s. Ho pleaded guilty and on 22 Sep 2026 was sentenced to 57 months in prison; the other three defendants are set for trial in the term beginning 6 Jan 2027.
