Notes, disputes and open questions

Exporting to China in breach of US controls is not itself a crime in Taiwan, so prosecutors used other charges. Prosecutors sought 5 years (the statutory maximum) for four defendants including the Nvidia manager, and between 18 months and 6 years overall; the 6-year request relates to the separate Albatron embezzlement count (CNA, LTN). Investigation began after the Coast Guard uncovered suspected exports; searches from 20 May 2026. Conduct start is approximate (buyer whitelisted Feb 2025). Per CNA and LTN, eight people are charged over the servers and one further person only in the related Albatron embezzlement part. Keelung District Court held preparatory hearings on 16 Oct and 16 Nov 2026; one defendant denied the charges (udn). No trial date reported.
