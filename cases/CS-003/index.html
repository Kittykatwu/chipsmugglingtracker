Defendant notes

  • Alan Hao Hsu: DOJ called him the first person charged and convicted in an AI diversion case. Plea agreement: up to 10 years on Count 1 (smuggling) and 5 years on Count 2, which may run consecutively. The government will ask that sentencing be deferred until his cooperation is complete.
  • Hao Global LLC: Faced a fine of up to twice the gross gain plus probation.
  • Benlin Yuan: Allegedly organized sham inspections and paid $1M to the FBI believing he was recovering seized chips (Houston Chronicle). Indicted 17 Dec 2025 with Gong (4:25-cr-00687). Trial dates of 2 Mar, 15 Jun and 3 Aug 2026 were vacated. On 9 Jun 2026 the court suppressed border-search evidence and his BIS interview statements; on 23 Jun 2026 it barred Hsu's testimony. The government appealed.
  • Fanyue 'Tom' Gong: Sentenced 31 Aug 2026 to time served, 3 years supervised release, $100 special assessment and no fine (docket minute entry); judgment entered 1 Sep 2026. Count 1 dismissed on the government's motion. No DOJ release found.

Notes, disputes and open questions

Payments were routed through intermediaries in Thailand, Singapore, Malaysia, China and Taiwan (The Wire China). Hsu and Hao Global pleaded guilty on 10 Oct 2025 (unsealed 8-9 Dec 2025); DOJ called it the first AI-diversion conviction. Hsu's sentencing has been reset several times (18 Feb, 5 Mar, 19 Aug and 16 Dec 2026) and is now set for 26 May 2027 on an agreed motion; his plea agreement provides that the government will ask for sentencing to be deferred until his cooperation is complete. The ~$50M recorded as reaching China comes from the Houston Chronicle; no official source stating a delivered value has been found. DOJ separately states more than $50M in wire transfers from the PRC and more than $50M in "Nvidia technologies and cash" seized. Hsu's maximum is 10 years on the smuggling count and 5 years on the export-information count, which may run consecutively (plea agreement); this explains the 10- vs 15-year figures in reporting. In Yuan's case the court suppressed certain evidence and barred Hsu's testimony in June 2026; the government has appealed to the Fifth Circuit (Nos. 26-20384, 26-20414).
