  • NJ Global Financial: Faces fine up to S$1M or twice the benefit, whichever is higher.

Notes, disputes and open questions

Singapore does not enforce other countries' export controls, so charges are for fraud. Prohibition-of-disposal order on a ~S$55M bungalow; ~S$1M seized (not converted). First charges 27 Feb 2025 (SPF). Next court date for newest defendants 4 Jan 2027. Do not assume defendant Li Ming is the same person as a 'Li Ming' named in reporting on Megaspeed. Singapore's home affairs minister said the servers went to Malaysia, "from Singapore to a country other than China," and "may contain Nvidia chips" (MHA transcript, Mar 2025). Victims named by SPF: Dell, Super Micro and Asus. The 4 Jan 2027 court date is from news reports.
